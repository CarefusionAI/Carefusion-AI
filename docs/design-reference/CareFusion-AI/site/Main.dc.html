Your record, your key

Nobody opens your record without you.

A doctor needs the key on your phone — or your health card and a code sent to your basic phone. Every view is logged, and you see the history.

  1. 1

    Show your rotating key

    A QR and a 6-digit number that change every 10 seconds, used once.

  2. 2

    One doctor, thirty minutes

    They can read and add notes, never edit or delete. You can end the session early.

  3. 3

    Emergencies need your contact

    If you can't share, your emergency contact approves on their phone. Break-glass access is reviewed twice.

  4. 4

    See who looked, and when

    Your access history lists every session. The log can't be edited by anyone.

AI that shows its work

Evidence first. The doctor decides.

Four tools — symptoms, medical images, genomics and vital-sign trends — propose hypotheses. Each is checked against guidelines and research, labelled VERIFIED, SUPPORTED WITH CAUTION or UNCERTAIN, and reviewed by a doctor before you ever see it.
